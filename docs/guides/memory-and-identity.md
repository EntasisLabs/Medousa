# Memory & identity

**Audience:** Medousa app users managing their profile and saved personal context.

Medousa keeps two related pictures:

| Layer | What it is |
|-------|------------|
| **Memory** | Context from chats, projects, and long threads |
| **Identity** | Saved information about you — preferences, people, and facts |

Essentials can surface at the start of a turn. You can add one fact at a time
or export the saved context as markdown.

---

## Settings → Memory

Open **Settings → Memory** to tune how long chats stay vivid, retention-style
charter options, and related rules. Exact controls evolve; the intent is: keep
what matters without drowning every turn in noise.

---

## Your profile and saved context

Open **You** to review your profile. Select a preference, person, or fact to see
its saved value. The profile summary shows counts of each type.

Use **Add information** to save a fact or preference, **Add person** to record
someone and their relationship to you, or **Edit** on a selected card to update
its saved value. No profile information is required to start chatting.

You can also add information in chat:

- *“Remember that Mario is my brother.”*
- *“I prefer concise answers unless I ask for depth.”*
- *“Call this profile work — shorter, more formal.”*

**Profiles** are hats on **one** brain (`work`, `home`, …) — not separate
accounts. Switch profile when the context changes; workshops are *which engine*,
profiles are *which hat* on that engine.

---

## Export and edit

Ask Medousa to export your identity / memory picture as markdown when you want
to review or edit offline. Hand an edited version back when you’re ready.

Power users can also use CLI identity helpers — see
[CLI & workspace](../cookbook/cli-and-workspace.md).

---

## Saved information

- Saved facts should come from information you provided or grounded conversation
  history.
- Tool and channel actions can still ask before acting — see **Settings → Reach**.

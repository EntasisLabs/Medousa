# Sharing and phone

Medousa can stay reachable on your LAN and pair with phone companions so the workshop is not trapped in one desktop window.

## Phone and LAN reachability

In Settings → Workshop (This Mac) and Settings → Sharing / Network bands you will find controls for:

- Whether the workshop answers on local Wi‑Fi
- How phones discover or connect
- Related trust / pairing state

Turn reachability on when you actually use a phone companion. Leave it off on untrusted networks.

## Shared surfaces

Sharing channels (when configured) let other devices participate without cloning your entire desk. Treat invites and codes like keys — short-lived, intentional.

## Pairing another computer

Paired portals appear under **Your workshops**. After trust is established, **Switch** puts Home on that engine. Vault and sessions follow the workshop — confirm you switched before editing notes.

## Operator checklist

| Before… | Confirm… |
|---------|----------|
| Leaving the house with phone automations | Engine login-start / reachability |
| Pairing a new machine | You meant to trust that host |
| Debugging "phone can't see Home" | Same network, workshop active, firewall |
